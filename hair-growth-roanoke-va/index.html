    How Hair Growth Treatment Works in Roanoke, VA

    Hair Loss Treatment for Men in Roanoke

    Most male hair loss is androgenetic alopecia. DHT, made from testosterone, gradually miniaturizes follicles in genetically sensitive men, starting at the temples or crown. Finasteride lowers DHT, and minoxidil supports growth directly. Used together, they usually beat either one alone.

    Hair Loss Treatment for Women

    In women, thinning usually shows up as a wider part or less volume on top, while the front hairline stays put. Female pattern hair loss is the most common cause, but thyroid problems, low iron, a recent pregnancy, crash dieting and major stress can all cause shedding that looks similar. Minoxidil is the main FDA-approved option for women. Some women add off-label spironolactone or low-dose oral minoxidil.

    Finasteride + Minoxidil in Roanoke

    Finasteride is an FDA-approved 1 mg daily tablet for male pattern hair loss, and it lowers scalp and blood DHT by roughly two-thirds. Minoxidil, FDA-approved as a topical, keeps follicles in the growth phase longer so strands come in thicker. The pairing is the standard. A compounded combination spray is another route, though it is not FDA-approved.

    Dutasteride + Minoxidil

    Dutasteride blocks both forms of the enzyme that makes DHT, so it lowers DHT by more than 90 percent in the blood. It is FDA-approved for an enlarged prostate and used off-label for hair loss, most often in men who did not get enough from finasteride after a year. It lingers. Because it stays in the body for months, providers screen more carefully. Minoxidil rounds it out.

    Minoxidil + GHK-Cu in Roanoke

    Some people want a topical-only plan with no DHT blocker. In this blend, minoxidil drives regrowth and GHK-Cu, a copper peptide, has early research suggesting it may help the scalp environment. It is compounded. It is not FDA-approved. It suits women, and men avoiding finasteride.

    How a Provider Chooses Between Treatments

    Your provider weighs sex, age, pattern, speed of loss and medical history. They also ask about pregnancy plans, worries about sexual side effects, past depression and whether you would rather take a pill or apply something to your scalp twice a day. Men with typical pattern loss often start on finasteride and minoxidil. Minoxidil is the usual first step for women.

    What to Expect Over Time in Roanoke

    Hair grows about half an inch a month. Visible change takes three to six months, and full results take about a year, so patience is part of the plan. Some people shed more in weeks two through eight of minoxidil, which usually means follicles are cycling into a new growth phase. Stopping treatment lets the loss resume.
